**Against published figures**

- Karnataka rice yield 2018-19 to 2022-23: 3,012–3,089 kg of rice per ha (RBI). At a 67% paddy-to-rice out-turn that is about 4.5–4.6 t/ha of paddy. The twin gives 3.96 ± 0.06 t/ha (conventional) and 4.24 ± 0.06 t/ha (built-in); kharif 4.69 ± 0.02, rabi 3.79 ± 0.01 (built-in).
- AWD against continuous flooding, all else equal: water -27.5% [-28.1, -26.9], yield +0.1% [+0.1, +0.1], methane -45.0%. Published: mild AWD cuts water use by 23.4% without a significant yield loss (Carrijo et al. 2017); IRRI and TNAU cite up to 25–30% less water; IPCC scales methane by 0.55 for multiple drainage (−45%); Balaine et al. (2019) measured −41% for safe AWD.
